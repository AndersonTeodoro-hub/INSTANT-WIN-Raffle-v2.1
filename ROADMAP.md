## 02 — LIVE NOW · Onchain Event Center

The lottery was the proof of concept. The Event Center is the product: infrastructure for **provably fair giveaways, airdrops and promotional campaigns** — for brands, communities and web3 projects. Campaigns anyone can create and anyone can enter, with the winner picked by VRF and the prize held by a verified contract until it is claimed.

- **Free entry for participants** — the creator funds the prize, entrants never pay
- **ERC-20, ERC-721 and ERC-1155 prize modules already deployed and verified** — including tokenized assets issued on Robinhood Chain
- **Built for real campaign scale** — from a small community giveaway to large-brand airdrops
- **Winner selection always by Chainlink VRF** — no admin can pick, change or block a winner
- **Same guarantees as the lottery** — immutable contract, pull-payment claims, nothing can ever be trapped
- **No wallet, no crypto knowledge needed to enter**
